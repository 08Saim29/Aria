Privacy & terms

  • Your chats are saved to your Aria account (Google Firebase) so they sync across your devices, with a copy in your browser. Settings stay in your browser.
  • Your messages are sent to Google's Gemini AI to write answers. With web search on, a search query goes to Tavily. Images are made by Cloudflare AI. Photos you attach go to Gemini and are not saved.
  • You are in control: delete one chat, delete all chats, or delete your account in Settings.
  • Aria can be wrong. Do not rely on it for medical, legal or financial decisions, and do not share passwords or sensitive personal details.
  • Fair use: do not use Aria for anything illegal or harmful. Daily limits apply.
